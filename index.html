About Levels Dances

Learn about our mission to make dance accessible to everyone

Your Journey to Dance Mastery Starts Here

Levels Dances is a YouTube channel dedicated to providing high-quality dance tutorials and choreography breakdowns. Our mission is to make dance accessible to everyone, regardless of skill level or background.

Founded by professional dancers with years of experience in the industry, we break down complex moves into easy-to-follow steps. From hip-hop to contemporary, we cover a wide range of dance styles to help you find your rhythm.

Join our community of over 100,000 dancers who are improving their skills and expressing themselves through movement. New videos every week!
